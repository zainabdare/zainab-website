Information Systems & Decision Sciences

Entering LSU, I realized that while I excelled at content, everything I truly loved doing lived behind the scenes in technology—how systems operate, managing structured data architecture, optimizing processes, and engineering technical workflows. Looking back, technology has always been the common denominator across my entire path—from digital building and architectural drafting to scaling online content, and now engineering systems as an Information Systems & Decision Sciences major.
