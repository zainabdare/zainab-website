How Did I Get Here?

Age 8 — Aspiring Teacher

Early on, I dreamed of being an educator, developing a natural passion for explaining complex concepts, mentoring others, and structuring knowledge.

Age 10 — The Artist

My interests evolved toward visual creativity and design. I was great artistically and won multiple state and scholastic art awards, building an eye for detail and composition.

Age 15 — Roblox Architect & Content Creator

During quarantine, I poured my creativity into Roblox Bloxburg, designing and architecting detailed virtual houses daily. I launched a YouTube channel documenting my builds that scaled to over 1,000 subscribers and generated over 1,000,000 views, sparking my fascination with digital systems and user interaction.

Age 16–18 — Architectural Drafting & Certifications

Turned digital building into real-world engineering through Houston Community College (HCC), earning my Certificate in Drafting & Design Engineering Technology and professional certifications in AutoCAD and Revit.

Age 18 — Digital Marketing

I was really good with content creation, scaling platforms to over 100K followers and generating over 32 million total views. That's why I initially chose digital marketing—because it was what I was naturally good at.

Present / College — Information Systems & Decision Sciences

Entering LSU, I realized that while I excelled at content, everything I truly loved doing lived behind the scenes in technology—how systems operate, managing structured data architecture, optimizing processes, and engineering technical workflows. That clarity led me to commit fully to Information Systems & Decision Sciences with an Analytics concentration.

Looking back, technology has always been the common denominator across my entire path—from digital building and architectural drafting to scaling online content, and now engineering systems as an Information Systems & Decision Sciences major.
